## 1. Getting Started with HBOS

### Q1: What is HBOS and what does it help my business manage?
**Simple Answer:**  
**HBOS** stands for **Hyperlocal Business Operating System**, developed by **SkyraSoft**. It is a **complete cloud software platform for running your retail store, supermarket, pharmacy, wholesale shop, or multi-branch business**.

Instead of using paper registers, sticky notes, or separate calculator apps, HBOS puts your entire shop onto your computer screen or tablet:
* **Checkout Counter (POS):** Fast live search, touch category selection, multi-tender payments (Cash, Card, Khata, Split), and instant receipt printing.
* **Store Inventory (Stock):** Shows you exactly how many items you have on your shelves right now and warns you before you run out.
* **Supplier Purchases:** Keeps track of orders from vendors and how much money you owe them.
* **Customer Khata (Udhaar Book):** Replaces your old credit diary so you always know who owes you money, how much they owe, and when they promised to pay.
* **Cash Drawer & Bank Money:** Tells you the exact cash in your cash drawer, money in the bank, and your daily profits after expenses.

---

### Q2: What are the main modules included in HBOS?
**Simple Answer:**  
Think of "Modules" as the different departments of your store, all working together on your screen:
1. **Point of Sale (POS):** Your checkout register where your cashiers search products, click category tiles, ring up customer items, apply discounts, and generate bills.
2. **Products & Catalog:** The master list of everything you sell, organized neatly by Name, Category, Subcategory, and Brand.
3. **Inventory & Stock:** Your virtual warehouse that counts stock going in and out and warns you when stock is low.
4. **Suppliers & Purchases:** Your vendor management area where you record incoming shipments and supplier payments.
5. **Customer Khata (Credit Book):** Your digital ledger for tracking credit customers, loan limits, and payment receipts.
6. **Cash & Bank (Money Accounts):** Your cash drawers, shop safes, and bank accounts tracking daily cashflow.
7. **Expenses:** Where you record daily shop costs like electricity bills, shop rent, staff tea, and salaries.
8. **Branches & Locations:** Where you manage multiple shop locations from one central screen.
9. **Staff & Users:** Where you create accounts for your workers and control what each person is allowed to see or do.
10. **Dashboard & Reports:** Summary cards, graphs, and profit/loss reports showing how healthy your business is.

### Q3: How are the different HBOS modules connected to each other?
**Simple Answer:**  
In HBOS, **everything happens automatically in real time in the cloud**. When you do something in one part of the system, all other parts update instantly without you having to re-type anything.

**Real-World Example:**
> Imagine a customer walks in and buys **1 bottle of Shampoo for Rs. 500 in cash**:
> 1. **At the POS Counter:** The cashier types "sham" in the search box (or clicks the *Haircare* category) and clicks Add to Cart. The bill is completed.
> 2. **In Stock (Inventory):** The system automatically subtracts 1 bottle of shampoo from your shelf stock count (e.g., from 20 down to 19).
> 3. **In the Cash Drawer:** The system automatically adds Rs. 500 to today's cash drawer balance.
> 4. **On the Owner's Dashboard:** Total Daily Sales increases by Rs. 500, and Net Profit increases by your profit margin on that bottle.
> 5. **In the Audit Log:** The system makes a permanent record stating: *"Cashier Ali sold 1 Shampoo to Walk-In Customer at 2:15 PM."*

You only had to click once — HBOS handled all the accounting in the background!

### Q5: What is the difference between a Business, Main Store, and Branch in HBOS?
**Simple Answer:**  
Think of HBOS like a family tree of your company:
* **1. Business (The Entire Company / Tenant):**  
  This is your registered company name (e.g., *"Al-Madina Trading Co."*). All your products, staff, bank accounts, and settings belong to this one big umbrella. Your data is 100% private and protected from all other companies.
* **2. Primary Branch (Main Store / Head Office):**  
  This is your main physical shop or warehouse where you first started. When you create your account, HBOS automatically creates this main branch for you.
* **3. Secondary Branches (Other Shop Locations):**  
  These are your additional shops (e.g., *"Branch 2 - Gulberg Mall"* or *"Warehouse B"*). Each branch has its own physical stock on shelves and its own cash drawer, but the Business Owner can see all of them together on one screen.

### Q6: What user roles are available in HBOS and what is each role allowed to do?
**Simple Answer:**  
HBOS comes with 3 standard roles so you can give your employees only the access they need to do their job:
1. **Business Owner (`owner`):**
   * The Big Boss. Has 100% full access to everything across all shops.
   * Can see total profits, bank accounts, change prices, add/remove staff, and create new branches.
2. **Branch Manager (`manager`):**
   * The Store Supervisor. Has full access to manage **their assigned branch only**.
   * Can make sales, record supplier purchases, adjust stock, record shop expenses, and view branch reports.
   * Cannot see other branches or delete the business.
3. **Salesperson / Cashier (`salesperson`):**
   * The Counter Worker. Fast and simple checkout screen only.
   * Can search products, select category tiles, add items to cart, take payments, and print receipts.
   * Cannot see your wholesale purchase cost, cannot see company profits, and cannot change product prices.

## 7. Inventory and Stock Management

### Q91: How does HBOS track stock quantities automatically when items are bought and sold?
**Simple Answer:**  
HBOS uses an **Automated Real-Time Stock Ledger**:
* **When you receive goods from a supplier:** Stock automatically goes **UP (+)**.
* **When a cashier sells an item at POS:** Stock automatically goes **DOWN (-)**.
* **When a customer returns an item:** Stock automatically goes **UP (+)**.
* **When you return damaged goods to a supplier:** Stock automatically goes **DOWN (-)**.
You never have to calculate or write stock numbers by hand!

### Q160: What is the Profit & Loss (P&L) Report and how is Net Profit calculated after cost of goods and expenses?
**Simple Answer:**  
The **P&L Report** is the single most important report for a business owner:
* **Gross Revenue (Sales):** Total money made from sales (e.g., Rs. 500,000).
* **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS):** What you paid suppliers to buy those goods (e.g., Rs. 400,000).
* **Gross Profit:** Rs. 500,000 - Rs. 400,000 = **Rs. 100,000**.
* **Operating Expenses:** Rent, electricity, salaries, tea (e.g., Rs. 40,000).
* **Net Clean Profit:** Rs. 100,000 - Rs. 40,000 = **Rs. 60,000 Pure Profit**.
HBOS calculates this automatically for any date range with zero accounting knowledge required!
