# HBOS Final V1 Product Blueprint & Transformation Contract

**Program**: SkyraSoft HBOS Small Retail Transformation Program  
**Phase**: Phase 1 — Prompt 3 of 3: Blueprint & Transformation Contract  
**Status**: AUTHORITATIVE FROZEN PRODUCT BLUEPRINT  

---

## 1. Product Mission & Non-Goals

- **Target Customer**: Small Pakistani retail shopkeeper (Single-store owner-operator + small multi-branch growth).
- **Non-Goals**: Not an enterprise ERP, General Ledger, corporate HR, or custom workflow platform.

## 2. Business Shapes & Active V1 Roles

- **Single-Store Owner-Operated**: 1 Business, 1 Main Store, 0 mandatory employees.
- **Small Multi-Branch**: Same Business, Main Store + one or more additional operational branches with progressive disclosure.
- **Active Roles**: Business Owner (UI: Business Super Owner), Branch Manager, Salesperson (UI: Cashier / Salesperson).
