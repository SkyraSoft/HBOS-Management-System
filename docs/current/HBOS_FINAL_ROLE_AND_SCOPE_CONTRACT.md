# HBOS Final Role & Scope Contract

## Active V1 Role Product Contracts

1. **Business Owner** (UI: Business Super Owner): Universal authority across All Stores; direct operations; read-only View-As mode.
2. **Branch Manager**: Scoped strictly to assigned branch; local inventory & petty cash; central treasury hidden.
3. **Salesperson** (UI: Cashier / Salesperson): POS checkout, customer Khata lookup, shift day close; cost prices & margins hidden.
